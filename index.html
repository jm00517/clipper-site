Pudding Clipper

A personal clip-editing tool. Not a public service.

What it is

Pudding Clipper is a private desktop tool used by a single person (the channel owner) to cut highlight clips from VTuber live-stream archives, add subtitles and simple effects, render the videos on their own PC, and upload the finished videos to their own YouTube channels.

How it uses the YouTube API

  • Upload (videos.insert): uploads a video the owner rendered, with the title, description and tags the owner entered.
  • Thumbnail (thumbnails.set): sets the custom thumbnail generated for that video.
  • Status (videos.list): checks the processing status and privacy of videos the tool uploaded.

OAuth scopes: youtube.upload, youtube.readonly. The tool only acts on the two channels owned by the operator:

  • プリン戦士 (Japanese) — @pudding-sensi
  • 푸링전사 (Korean) — UCNh6vEwAXp_2YKaA_555LAQ

There are no other users. The tool does not access, store or share any other YouTube user's data.
